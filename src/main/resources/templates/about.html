The Origins

Originally conceived as an underground digital proving ground hidden deep within the university network architecture, CRUD CTF has evolved into a premier technical and psychological gauntlet. We don't just test your ability to read code; we test how you respond when the system fights back.

The Philosophy

The internet is a house of cards held together by legacy code and blind trust. Here, you will learn to exploit both. Our challenges are designed around the core principles of data manipulation: Create, Read, Update, and Destroy.

Expect nothing to be as it seems. What looks like a simple forensic analysis might be a trap; what looks like a broken script might be a backdoor. Leave your assumptions at the login screen.

Rules of Engagement

  • No Denial of Service (DoS): Do not attempt to take down the competition infrastructure. If you crash the server, you lose.
  • Keep Flags Confidential: Sharing flags with competing teams will result in an immediate, unappealable ban from the network.
  • Report Infrastructure Bugs: If you find a zero-day in our actual platform rather than a challenge, report it to the admins. You may be rewarded.
  • Trust No One: Not your teammates, not the challenge descriptions, and especially not the system logs.
